01THE OVERVIEW

Exploring AI
for visual workflows.

A collection of generative AI experiments for AEC visual workflows, showing how prompts, sketches, plans, and model inputs can be processed into images and video. The focus is on AI tooling and workflow exploration.

01

Text → render

Conceptual scenes from prompts: residential village, cafes, entries, interiors.

02

Sketch → render

Freehand sketches conditioned through ControlNet to produce coherent architectural variations.

03

Model → render

Massing and model exports re-rendered into day and night photoreal scenes.

04

Plan → render

Floor plans expanded into rendered interiors and elevations.

05

AI video

Cabin construction, shopping mall, residential village, and morning residence walkthroughs.
