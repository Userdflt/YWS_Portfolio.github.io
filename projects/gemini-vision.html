01THE OVERVIEW

An idea with
a practical purpose.

Vision Studio is a multi-agent image-generation tool that converts a one-line brief into a structured prompt, then routes it to specialist agents — inpainting, floorplan layout, scene composition — powered by Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash.

The challenge

Most image tools take raw prompts and deliver one-shot results. Architecture and design briefs need a structured pipeline — context, references, intent — before a useful image can be produced.

01

Brief → prompt agent

Expands a sparse brief into a detailed, model-ready prompt with style, materials, lighting.

02

Specialist routing

Different agents handle inpainting, plan generation, and free-form scenes.

03

Local persistence

IndexedDB stores sessions, references, and history without a backend.
