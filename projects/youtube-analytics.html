01THE OVERVIEW

An idea with
a practical purpose.

A statistical study of AI / data-science content on YouTube. Data collected via the YouTube API, cleaned, explored, and tested for temporal patterns and engagement shifts across cohorts using ANOVA and follow-up post-hoc tests.

The challenge

Anecdotal evidence suggested the AI content boom changed the shape of educational YouTube — but by how much, and across which dimensions? A rigorous study, not vibes.

01

API ingestion + cleaning

Collected channel and video metadata; normalised across time and category.

02

EDA → hypotheses

Visualised temporal patterns; formed testable hypotheses about engagement shifts.

03

ANOVA + post-hoc

Tested cohort differences; reported effect sizes alongside p-values.

02HOW IT WORKS

From input
to insight.

  1. 01

    Collect

    YouTube API — key setup, search-result retrieval for data-science/AI queries.

  2. 02

    Clean

    Nulls, duplicates, unnecessary columns; date extraction and feature creation.

  3. 03

    EDA

    View/like/comment distributions (histograms, KDE); per-category totals; yearly + monthly trend time series.

  4. 04

    Hypothesis test

    Skewness + kurtosis checks, then ANOVA across years (H0: mean view/like/comment counts equal across years).

  5. 05

    Conclude

    Statistically significant changes over recent years; overall decrease in trend.
